Simulating SPA Client-Side Route Handling

In SPAs (React, Vue, or Vanilla), deep routes like /dashboard or /profile do not physically exist on disk. AWS Amplify uses Redirects & Rewrites to transparently serve /index.html with a 200 (Rewrite) status code.
